Getting Started

Lay out a vertiport's surfaces from the FAA draft Advisory Circular in a few steps. No sign-in needed.

  1. Place the padSearch an address, or click the map where the pad goes. Drag the blue pin to fine-tune it. Ground elevation fills in on its own.
  2. Describe the aircraftPick Powered lift or Helicopter. Choose a helicopter preset, or type the aircraft's D, RD and weight from the manufacturer.
  3. Aim the approachesDrag the numbered handle at the end of each blue surface, or type the course. Turn on Reciprocal for a straight-through path.
  4. Check the resultsRead the sizes under Results and any Notes. Switch to 3D View to see the surfaces over the terrain.
  5. ExportDownload a KML for Google Earth or a GeoJSON for GIS. Save the design file to reopen it later.

Tap any button for an explanation of that setting.
